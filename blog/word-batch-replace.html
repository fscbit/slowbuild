The Pain of Manual Document Filling

You have one Word template and a list of 100 clients. Every file needs the same edits — swap in the name, the date, the amount — but each one is slightly different. Doing it by hand means opening 100 files and running find-and-replace three times each. That is an entire morning gone.

How the Batch Replace Tool Works

Instead of editing documents one by one, you mark the changing parts of your template as placeholders like #Name# or #Date#. Then you prepare a spreadsheet where each row is one client. Upload the template and the sheet, and the tool fills every placeholder with the matching column value, producing one clean Word document per row.

Step 1: Prepare a Word template with #placeholder# markers.
Step 2: Make an Excel/CSV table with one row per record and matching column headers.
Step 3: Upload both, click generate, and download the finished files.
